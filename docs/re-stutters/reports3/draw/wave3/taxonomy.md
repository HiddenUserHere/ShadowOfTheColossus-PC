## 0. Method and limits

- Frames: 158 unique frames (de-duplicated by frame start time: the dumps overlap). 22 frames are not inside
  the window of any dump that holds them and are dropped. **136 covered frames**: 38 normal (unflagged, under
  40 ms), 39 mid (unflagged, 40 ms and up), 59 slow (flagged by the recorder, 50 ms and up).
- Draw-thread time is split by **self time** (a span's time minus its nested spans), so the parts add up to the
  covered time. Nested overlaps clipped: 0.
- **Floors of the recording**: ops under 50 us are merged into one `ops` span (`a` = count, `op` for one);
  `ScopeMin` spans and the RENDERER zones (`Draw`, `DrawIndirect`, `DispatchDirect`, `DispatchIndirect`)
  drop anything under 20 us. So ~4,600 ops per normal frame are mostly invisible: their time is the self time of
  `ops`. Per-kind op costs are therefore lower bounds; the untraced op body is the biggest unknown.
- Categories (the UMA question). A span is EMU when it is emulation of unified memory: coherence sync, page
  protect and copies (`sync *`, `obtain buffer`), `RefreshImage`/`image upload`, `invalidate images`, write
  faults, readbacks (`rb *`), upload fences, `wait tc lock`/`wait upload`. MIXED is descriptor work that also
  carries coherence checks (`bind resources/buffers/textures`, `find image`), flow waits (`wait gpu`,
  `wait recorder`), `hle fill`, and the **untraced op body** (self time of `ops`, `op`, the long-op wrappers and
  the zones). RENDER is real work (`get pipeline`, `render state`, `begin rendering`, `vertex buffers`,
  `indirect args`, `command`, `eop/release`). IDLE is `draw idle`.

## 1. Headline

- The draw thread is busy 96-98% of every group (covered 32.6 / 43.9 / 57.8 ms of 33.6 / 45.2 / 59.5 ms frames).
  Its waits on other threads total under 2 ms per slow frame (`wait gpu` 1.05, `wait tc lock` 0.48, `wait upload` 0.14,
  `wait recorder` 0.07). The draw thread never waits on the GPU, recorder or uploads to any real degree.
- The other threads wait on the draw thread: the game threads sit in `evf wait` / `equeue wait` for the whole
  frame in slow frames (`RenderFlip` 59.5 ms, `Game Render` 59.5 ms), and the GPU is 33% busy in normal frames
  (cmdbuf track 11.1 ms of 33.6) and 29% in slow frames (16.9 ms of 59.5). The port is CPU-bound on the draw thread.
- Slow minus normal: +25.2 ms of covered draw time per frame (32.6 -> 57.8 ms covered; frame +25.9 ms). Of that,
  the untraced `ops` body is +12.3 ms, traced EMU is +4.2 ms, the other traced MIXED work (bind, zones, waits) is
  +7.8 ms, RENDER +0.5, IDLE +0.5 (these sum to the covered delta).
- **UMA emulation share**: traced EMU is only 4.4 ms of 32.6 covered in normal frames (13%) and 8.6 ms of 57.8 in
  slow frames (15%). The untraced op body is where most of the rest sits. The estimate in section 7 puts the total
  UMA share at roughly 35-40% of the draw thread (range 13-60%). The estimate is not measured; section 7 says how
  to measure it.

## 7. UMA emulation share: estimate

Traced EMU: 4.4 ms normal, 8.6 ms slow, which is 13-15% of covered draw time. The big part is the untraced op
body (20.8 ms normal, 33.1 ms slow, self time of `ops`). Earlier measurements (`DRAW-THREAD-DESIGN.md` §1.2, a profile
of the same route, measured earlier, not this session) attribute the largest share of op execution to bind-path
coherence: `SynchronizeMemory` 15.5%, DMA sweeps 4.5%, re-protect 6.8%, texture refresh/overlap/copy 4.3+3.9+3.6%,
CPU copy shaders 3.3%, readback chain 5.7+5.7+3.3%, all as shares of the frame. These overlap (page scans sit inside
`SynchronizeMemory`), so they cannot be added.

Working estimate, labelled as such: EMU = traced 4.4 ms + about 40% of the untraced body (20.8 ms) = about 13 ms of
33.6 ms normal (~38%); slow: 8.6 + 40% of 33.1 = about 22 ms of 59.5 ms (~37%). Range: 13% (traced only) to ~60%
(all of the untraced body that is bind-path). the rest (RENDER plus the real part of MIXED) is then about 21 ms normal and about 37 ms slow.

**To measure it** (not run, no game run in this task): `SOTC_OP_PHASES=1` (already in the code, `sotc_op_phases.h`)
splits each op into pipeline / render state / bind (clean, dirty, written buffers; textures) / begin rendering /
descriptor push / dynamic state / HLE / reset. Run it with `SOTC_TRACE=1 SOTC_TRACE_ZONES=1` on the same route, then
the untraced body is attributed. Or lower `kMinNs` in `sotc_trace.h` for one run.

## 9. Surprises

1. The per-kind split of the draw thread is mostly unrecorded: 20 ms of 33 ms in normal frames is `ops` self time.
2. The slow-frame extra is op count (+27%) times per-op cost (+38%): both matter; neither alone explains it.
3. `sync copy` costs about 1.5 ms per frame in every group. The byte volume is what varies (dma 3.5 -> 6.5 MiB mean,
   area-load frames up to 25 MB p95). The copy time does not, so the bytes are not the bottleneck; the op count is.
4. The GPU is 29-33% busy in every group (cmdbuf). The watcher `gpu` spans say 60-70%: they are not a GPU measure.
5. The CP is not recorded in a usable way (0.3 ms per frame). Its wait on the draw thread cannot be read from the trace.
6. 22 of 158 unique frames are only partly inside any dump window: a dump keeps about 150 ms before the slow frame,
   so frames that start earlier are cut. Those were dropped.
7. The guest `readback` spans carry the size in `b`, not a caller. The guest caller of a readback is not recorded.
8. One host RIP (`0x7ff60f024742`) takes all 822 draw-thread write faults in the slow frames. The symbol is not
   resolved (binary mismatch).
